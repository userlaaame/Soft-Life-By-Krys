Growing up in NJ, the closest thing to a vacation you get on a staycation is the Jersey Shore. But I needed a break from work and an escape from the hustle of life, and that's how I found Frenchtown. This small town sits along the Delaware River and is full of restaurants, boutique shops, art, cafés, and museums.

## Coffee First

Before exploring anything, I love to find a café and grab a coffee. We stopped in at **Earlybird** for a coffee and a tea. The shop was very cozy and even had a Mediterranean-scented candle that smelled absolutely divine. I went back and bought it later.

## Exploring the Town

Next, we went to a museum that had opened that very day. Then we made our way to the bookstore before finishing the day with dinner.

## Where to Eat

There are several amazing restaurants, including an Italian spot that's also BYOB. You can also grab a bite at the **National Hotel** restaurant and finish off with a delicious cocktail.
