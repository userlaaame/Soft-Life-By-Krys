I'd heard a lot about Sedona namely, that it's one of the few vortexes in the world. Many people who live in or visit Sedona are deeply spiritual and come for its healing, positive energy. As someone who was definitely on the spiritual path but sort of fell off, I can understand the hype.

## Our First Stop

On our move from LA to NJ, Sedona was our first stop, and driving through it literally felt like entering another dimension. The red rocks made it feel like we were driving on another planet.

## Where We Stayed: Amara Resort

Believe it or not, Sedona isn't cheap to live in or visit, but I was able to find a reasonably priced resort: **Amara Resort**. What I loved most is that it sits right along a creek you can actually swim in. When we finished in the creek, we made our way to the infinity pool, which overlooks the brook.

The resort is also within walking distance of numerous stores and restaurants, and strolling through them feels like walking through a Disney town. The mountain views are especially breathtaking.

## Exploring the Town

We spent the day walking through town and grabbed a smoothie before making our way to **Tlaquepaque Arts & Crafts Village**. It was the cutest little shopping center, with boutique artisan shops, cafés, and ice cream — it looked like it came right out of a Disney movie.

## Final Thoughts

We were only in Sedona for 48 hours, but it left a lasting mark. We'll definitely be back soon.
