Why is there so much hype around Joshua Tree? I mean, it's literally just a bunch of trees and desert… right?

Well, technically, yes. But if you live in California, you know that's kind of the point.

Joshua Tree is one of those places that makes the perfect staycation. It's relatively affordable, easy to get to, and somehow makes you feel like you've completely left California and entered another world.

## Where to Stay

When you need a break from your normal routine, it's not too hard to find a reasonably priced Airbnb. You can stay closer to the main road, near the neighboring towns and restaurants, or go completely off the grid and find a secluded place surrounded by nothing but desert.

Personally, I prefer the secluded option — ideally with a pool and a firepit.

There's something about having your own little desert getaway where you can spend the afternoon by the pool, then sit around a firepit at night, wrapped in a blanket with a drink, watching the stars. It's the kind of simple luxury that makes the whole trip feel like an actual escape.

## What to Do

And if you're wondering, *"Okay, but what is there actually to do in Joshua Tree?"*

Honestly? Not much.

And that's exactly the point.

There's something so nice about having nowhere to be and nothing to do. You can sleep in, sit by the pool, sip your coffee slowly, watch the sunset, and just take in the quiet beauty of the desert. It's the kind of place where doing absolutely nothing suddenly feels like the perfect plan.

That said, you're not completely cut off from civilization. There's a surprisingly good selection of restaurants, cafés, and places to grab a drink.

During the day, stop by **Mas o Menos** for coffee, then find somewhere to grab drinks in the evening. And if you really want to lean into the whole Joshua Tree experience, I highly recommend a warm spring bath around sunset.

## The Night Sky

But my favorite part?

The night sky.

If you can, plan your trip around a new moon, when the skies are clear. With very little light pollution, the stars are absolutely incredible. Grab a drink, sit around the firepit, and look up — suddenly you understand why people keep coming back to Joshua Tree.

---

Maybe it really is just a bunch of trees and desert.

But sometimes, that's exactly what you need.
